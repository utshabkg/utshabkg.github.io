Asguard

Car and home maintenance in one app.

Asguard keeps track of what your car and your home need, then reminds you before anything is overdue. Tell it your make, model and year and it loads the factory service schedule for that exact car. Add your home and it fills in the filters, alarms, gutters and seasonal jobs nobody remembers on their own.

  • No accountNothing to sign up for. Open it and use it.
  • Works offlineEverything runs on your phone, with no connection needed.
  • Your data stays on your deviceNo servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Real schedulesFactory intervals for popular models, and every one of them editable.
  • Backups you controlExport whenever you want, or have it back up automatically to a folder you choose.

Your owner's manual is always the authority. Asguard gives you starting points and remembers it for you. See the Terms for the full version.
