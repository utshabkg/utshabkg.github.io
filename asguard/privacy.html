Asguard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

Short version: Asguard does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Asguard ("the app") is an offline car and home maintenance tracker. It is designed with privacy as a core feature: there is no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no analytics.

What data the app stores

Everything you create in Asguard is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your assets (cars, homes) and their details
  • Maintenance tasks, schedules, and due dates
  • Completion records, including dates, costs, notes, and odometer readings
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach
  • App settings

This information never leaves your device through the app. We have no servers, and we never receive, see, or store your data.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not transmit your data off your device.
  • We do not share or sell data to anyone.
  • We do not use advertising, ad networks, or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not require an account or an internet connection to use the app.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to remind you when maintenance is due.
  • Run at startup — so your reminders are rescheduled after your phone restarts.
  • Vibrate — for reminder notifications.

The app requests no camera permission and no photo/media permission. When you add a receipt photo, the app uses your device's built-in system photo picker or camera. Any photo you attach is copied into the app's private storage on your device. See Device (OS-level) backup below for the one case in which recent receipt photos are included in your own Google account backup.

Backups you create

Asguard lets you export a backup file containing your data and receipt photos. This file is created only when you ask for it, and it is handed to you (for example, to save to your own storage or share to your own cloud drive). The app does not upload it anywhere on its own. Once you export a file, how it is stored is under your control.

Automatic backup to a folder you choose

You can optionally choose a folder on your device for Asguard to write backups into automatically. If you do:

  • You pick the folder yourself, and Android grants Asguard access only to that folder.
  • Backups are written directly to that folder on your device. Asguard has no server and never sends the file to us or to any third party.
  • If the folder you choose is synchronised by another app (for example a cloud storage app), that app's own terms and privacy policy govern what happens to the file from there.

You can stop automatic backups at any time in Settings, which also releases Asguard's access to that folder.

Device (OS-level) backup

Depending on your Android device's own backup settings, the Android operating system may include the app's data in the standard device backup to your personal Google account. This is a function of your device and your Google account settings, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Asguard has no server and never sends your data to us or to any third party.

You should know exactly what that includes, because Asguard makes a deliberate choice here:

  • Your records (assets, tasks, service history, fuel log and settings) are included, so they come back when you install Asguard on a new phone.
  • Your receipt photos are excluded from that backup by default, because Android limits how much an app may store and exceeding it would cause the whole backup — records included — to be skipped.
  • So that your photos are not simply lost with a broken phone, Asguard writes a size-capped bundle of your most recent receipt photos (up to about 12 MB) into the data Android backs up. Those photos therefore travel to your own Google account along with your records, and are restored automatically on a new phone. Older photos beyond that limit are not included.
  • You can see exactly what is and is not covered at any time under Settings › If you lost this phone today, and you can protect every photo at full quality by choosing a backup folder.

All of this goes to your Google account, not ours. You can turn the device backup off entirely in your phone's backup settings.

Children's privacy

Asguard is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
